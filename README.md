# The Socioeconomic Geography of Electric Vehicle Adoption in Norway

## Research question

Are socioeconomic characteristics associated with differences in electric vehicle (EV) adoption across Norwegian municipalities, and how do these patterns differ between Northern Norway and the rest of the country?

## Project overview

This project examines municipal differences in the adoption of fully electric passenger cars in Norway. It aims to explore how EV adoption is associated with income, educational attainment, age structure, and population density.

The analysis will focus on 2024 and compare municipalities in Northern Norway with municipalities elsewhere in the country.

## Planned data sources

- **Statistics Norway (SSB):** Vehicle registrations by fuel type and municipality.
- **Statistics Norway (SSB):** Municipal socioeconomic and demographic indicators.
- **Statistics Norway (SSB):** Potential supplementary data on charging infrastructure, subject to data availability and compatibility.

The final analysis will document the selected tables, definitions, and data-cleaning decisions.

## Methods

The planned analysis includes:

- Data cleaning and merging at the municipality level.
- Descriptive statistics and visualisations.
- Comparisons between Northern Norway and the rest of the country.
- Correlation analysis.
- Multiple linear regression.

The results will be interpreted as associations, not necessarily causal effects.
